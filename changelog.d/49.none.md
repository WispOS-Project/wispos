**Changelog fragments replace the hand-edited Unreleased section (tooling, no SemVer effect; #49, from the #45 spike):** a change now adds `changelog.d/<issue>.<class>.md` (class `major`, `minor`, `patch` or `none`) instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md`, so concurrent PRs no longer conflict on the same lines. `tools/changelog.py` validates the fragments (`check`), names the bump they call for (`highest`) and assembles a dated release section (`release VERSION DATE`); the manifest version and the tag stay with the maintainer. Existing Unreleased entries are kept as they are.
